OUR STORY
ROOTED IN CARE.
MADE TO RADIATE.

SonKEI Co. is a family-owned beauty and wellness brand born from a mother’s love and a daughter’s personal journey. After watching my daughter struggle with severe teenage acne and harsh, chemical-filled products, I set out to create a pure, 100% chemical-free alternative. What began as a personal mission to heal her skin quickly blossomed into SonKEI Co.—a brand dedicated to nurturing your mind, body, and soul.

Today, we offer a holistic approach to wellness through three natural pillars: gentle, nutrient-rich facial skincare, mineral-packed wellness beverages like Sea Moss and natural juices, and motivational apparel designed to inspire and uplift your daily life. At SonKEI Co., we believe true beauty is a reflection of overall health, and we are committed to helping you look, feel, and live your absolute best—naturally.
