WHAT IS SEA MOSS

An ocean superfood, generations in the making.

Sea moss (Chondrus crispus and related species, sometimes called Irish moss) is a nutrient-dense algae that grows along rocky Caribbean and North Atlantic coastlines. It has been used for generations across Caribbean and coastal households as a whole-body wellness ritual — a natural source of iodine, potassium, calcium, sulfur compounds and antioxidants that support energy, digestion, skin and immunity. At SONKEI CO., ours is wild crafted from the clean coastal waters of St Lucia and sun dried the traditional way, then blended into a smooth gel with nothing added and nothing taken away.

HOW IT'S HARVESTED

From the coast of St Lucia to your jar.

Our Sea Moss is wild crafted by hand along the clean coastal waters of St Lucia, then laid out and sun dried the traditional way — no machines, no shortcuts. Once fully dried, it's thoroughly cleaned to remove sand and debris, then soaked and blended into the smooth, mineral-rich gel you receive.

This slow, traditional process is what keeps the mineral content intact and honors the way Caribbean communities have prepared Sea Moss for generations.

HOW TO USE

Simple to add to your daily ritual.

  1. Take 1–2 tablespoons daily, straight from the jar.
  2. Blend into smoothies, juices or oatmeal for an easy mineral boost.
  3. Stir into warm tea or water as a gentle daily tonic.
  4. Refrigerate after opening and use within 3–4 weeks.

DIY Face Mask

For a soothing, hydrating mask at home:

  1. Mix 1 tablespoon Sea Moss Gel with 1 teaspoon raw honey.
  2. Apply a thin layer to clean, dry skin, avoiding the eye area.
  3. Leave on for 10–15 minutes, then rinse with warm water.
  4. Use 1–2 times per week and follow with your favorite SONKEI CO. moisturizer.

DIY Hair & Scalp Mask

For added shine and a nourished scalp:

  1. Massage 2–3 tablespoons of Sea Moss Gel into the scalp and through lengths.
  2. Cover with a shower cap and leave on for 20–30 minutes.
  3. Rinse thoroughly, then shampoo and condition as usual.
  4. Repeat weekly for best results.
